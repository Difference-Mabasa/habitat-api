4. Terms

The Landlord hereby grants the Agent an exclusive mandate to act on their behalf in the letting and management of the Property described above, in line with the elected mandate type. The Agent will source and screen tenants, manage applications, collect rent into Habitat's trust account, and account to the Landlord in accordance with PPRA rules.

The Agent's fee, set out in section 3, is calculated on rent collected and deducted before payout. Either party may terminate this mandate on 30 days' written notice; active leases survive termination and continue to be managed until renewal.

Both parties confirm they have read and understood this agreement and agree to be bound by it. Where this mandate is signed offline, the Landlord's signature below is the binding instrument; the Agent attests on the platform that they hold the signed original.
